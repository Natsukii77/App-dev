# My Favorite Game: Honkai: Star Rail

## Overview
**Honkai: Star Rail** is a turn-based space-fantasy RPG made by *HoYoverse*. You play as the **Trailblazer**, a traveler who boards the **Astral Express** and journeys across different worlds, uncovering mysteries along the way.

## Why I Love It
- Beautiful art style and music
- Strategic turn-based combat
- Interesting worlds and storylines
- Characters with unique personalities

## Main Characters
1. Trailblazer
2. March 7th
3. Dan Heng
4. Himeko
5. Welt

## Worlds You Can Explore
- Herta Space Station
- Jarilo-VI
- The Xianzhou Luofu
- Penacony

## Combat Paths
| Path | Role |
|------|------|
| Destruction | Deals heavy damage |
| Harmony | Boosts the team |
| Preservation | Protects allies |
| Abundance | Heals the team |
